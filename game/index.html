A DUEL OF THE DEEP · 1 v 1 CARD BATTLE

TIDEBREAK

Three lanes. One rising tide. Thirty life between you and the Queen of the Abyss.

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Bubbles are mana. You gain +1 max bubble each turn (up to 10) and draw a card.
  2. Summon: click a card in your hand, then click a lane — Shallows, Reef or Abyss.
  3. Attack: click one of your glowing creatures, then an enemy creature or Queen Morvain herself.
  4. High tide: one lane each turn gives its creatures +2 attack. Fight where the tide is high.
  5. Swift creatures attack the turn they are played. Bulwark shields block the first hit.
  6. Win by bringing Queen Morvain to 0 life. Lose yours, and the deep keeps you.

An original game — its own creatures, art, sounds and rules. No account, no network, just the tide.
